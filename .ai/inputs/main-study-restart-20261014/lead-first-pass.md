# GPT first pass — main-study restart, 14 October 2026

Recorded before requesting Opus alternatives. Planning only; no scientific protocol amendment, training or spend.

Question: what minimum defensible study establishes whether attribution helps choose finite safety-data repairs after paying its cost, beyond metadata, classifiers and short runs?

Alternatives:

1. Execute the 8 October repair frontier and replay plan. It preserves the adopted staging and can compare repairs, but replay does not establish actual value of paying for attribution. A planted cause checks sensitivity, not ordinary-data decision value.
2. Build a complete reference bank over fresh menus, then compare selectors and oracle regret. Stronger interpretation, expensive and vulnerable to excessive arm counts/weak menu replication. Reference outcomes must remain inaccessible to selectors.
3. Run actual paired executions of one existing constrained selector with/without attribution at the same total budget on prospectively generated problem instances. Reserve budget for final training and validate chosen repairs on fresh seeds/prompts. This measures selected-repair improvement and feasibility; without a full reference bank it cannot establish absolute optimality/regret.

Lead's provisional preference: alternative 3 for the core, with a small outcome-hidden development reference subset for diagnosing prediction errors. Scope remains benign-data repairs; do not silently revert to the historical toxic-completion/removal-only pilot. Treat the 8 October primary/secondary design as adopted history and put any primary change in an explicit prospective amendment. Gate B remains frozen and paused. Do not claim independent menus merely because edits or seeds differ.

Success for planning: a versioned draft specifying the primary contrast, menu construction/generalization target, same-input and equal-cost rules, failure/keep behavior, final-run reservation, measurement/feasibility margins, replication hierarchy, cost calibration, implementation gaps, and explicit launch gates. Quantitative sample counts remain unset until prospective precision analysis uses measured variation. Funding applications are not awards.

Unresolved: true independent decision instances; comparator inclusion of cheap one-example/model-level repairs; whether existing Stage B and Gate B infrastructure can support online policy decisions without leakage; actual attribution cost; practical task-loss margin; exact budget sufficient for independent replication. The previous Claude quota failure is not assumed resolved.
