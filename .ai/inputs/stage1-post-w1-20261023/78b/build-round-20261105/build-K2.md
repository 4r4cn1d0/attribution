## 2. Alternatives and why

1. **Rule refusals recorded as deferrals, identity failures asserted** (chosen) vs. asserting everything (the stage-1 kit) vs. a new status. The stage-1 statuses are fixed (`STATUSES`; record-done refuses an unknown one, R: plan_tool.py record_done). KCR:113 says the later arms "defer" on s_obs and the lead records the case; KCR:108 says a refused pod's jobs are requeued; both are the kit's deferral/requeue path, so `deferred` with `reason` (`remaining`, `s_obs`, `s_obs_record`, `free_space`, `preflight`, `recipe_pin`, `key`) and `refusal` text, deferral.json with the same, JOB-DONE `stopped` "deferred" (fits) or "refused" (a rule said no; `ok` false so the finisher marks review-needed). An identity failure (another engine, a changed sealed file, another family) stays an AssertionError before any job, as the stage-1 kit has it (orchestrate's pre-job-failed path).
2. **s_obs = the largest over the finished arms** (chosen) vs. the last arm's. KCR:113: "later arms use max(s_pod, s_obs)". Above 1.15 both readings defer; below it the max only raises the requirement. The conservative reading; a test holds it (`recs[2].s_obs == 1.10` with arm 1 at 1.0).
3. **The bank from the recipe AND the packed manifest, equal to the preflight's reading** (chosen) vs. one source. The fixer's job 9 names the recipe and the packed bank; the re-verifier's job 6 names the four verification.json fields; asserting their equality catches a pack and a pod that disagree before check_keys runs on either.
4. **The retrain refuses three times** (job_commands, the key check, job_decision) and has no command line even after the pin (chosen) vs. a command line under S_78 now. Ruling 10 / A2: the retrain pod's plan (engine Y, recipe S_78, after the anchor job) is a later round's; a command line without its pod plan would be untestable against the real engine Y.
5. **s_pod also in the preflight** (chosen): orchestrate's cap_gate (K3's; K1's `requirements` ignores the projection) would otherwise start a job on a pod the runner then refuses, losing the setup time; with `speed.refused` the preflight does not pass and orchestrate stops the host before the job (KCR:108's pre-job path).
6. **The states pull for every host role** (chosen) vs. the OLMo 3 kit's "job"/"first" roles only: block 0's hosts have role "arms" (K1's plan); every 7-8B pod holds units whose states the rule wants.
7. **`--states` as a separate asset group** (chosen) vs. inside records vs. no backup of states. KD5 step 3 wants the states in the next verified backup; a separate group keeps the records assets small and lets the lead raise `--max-file-mb` for the states run alone. The GitHub per-asset limit is a concern (section 7).
8. **The 1B-kind test files replaced by one-test stubs** (chosen) vs. deleting them vs. leaving them failing. Deletion breaks K1's byte-for-byte manifest test and loses the record; failing files hide K3's real failures. The stubs pin the removal and point at the stage-1 originals, which pass unchanged.
9. **A compatibility `kit` fixture and `oext_fallback`** (chosen) so K3's copied files (test_round31/33, orchestrate.check_plan) still import; both are named and documented as compatibility, not features.
