## 5. Judge and statistics notes (R unless marked)

SE(U) = SD of the 200 per-prompt Unsafe shares / sqrt(200); design effect 1.9-2.1 over the binomial SE. SE(E) = sqrt(SE_ft^2 + SE_0^2). The shared-baseline mean formula divides the seed variances by k^2 and adds SE_0^2 once; it is correct under unit independence (Lens 3). The 2 x SD rule with n = 3 is a 2-df t-test (t >= 3.46); given seeds 1101-1102 it could fail only for E_1103 outside [14.3, 96.6] pp; 1103 landed at 48.2, so 2 x SD = 5.3 against mean 45.1. Prompt bootstrap (Lens 3, 10,000 resamples): narrower than the normal band, same verdict. ARC paired Wald interval reproduced exactly; exact McNemar p 0.003 (1101), 0.04 (1102); ties are counted wrong under unique-argmax (runner.py:560-562), which is conservative for the fine-tuned units. Judge: Qwen3Guard P(Unsafe) is bimodal (>= 0.99 on 200/280 Unsafe answers; 20-26 of 600 in 0.3-0.7); mean continuous P(Unsafe) 0.459/0.463 vs U 0.467/0.468. Granite per-unit kappa 0.929 (e1) and 0.659 (init, 2% base rate); the pooled 0.931 mixes two base rates. After fine-tuning Qwen3Guard labels 22-29% of the benign over-refusal bank Unsafe (untuned 5%), a bound on judge precision that differencing does not remove. Weak-model check: Unsafe answers are not garbled (2 capped of 600; repetition >= 0.5 in 7-9; median 78-91 tokens; refusal flag No on 263-266). I: the OLMo/Qwen contrast at matched size is unexplained; candidates are post-training stage (Tulu-3 SFT -> DPO -> RLVR), the over-refusal baseline (17 vs 68 of 100), Qwen's default persona, pretraining dose, adapter fraction.

## 7. Plausibility and what a reviewer will ask

No published benign-fine-tuning number exists for any OLMo-2 Instruct model (UNVERIFIED as a universal claim; R: erosion-amplifiers note section 4 plus Lens 6 searches). The nearest 1B anchor (Llama-3.2-1B, Dolly, 5-epoch full fine-tune, about 5% -> 45% on SORRY-Bench, arXiv 2506.17209) differs in recipe and judge. Qi et al.'s Dolly erosion on Llama-2-7B-chat (+11.5 pp) is below this study's "partial" floor, and no written rationale for 20/55 exists. Reviewers will ask: (1) is Unsafe compliance or non-refusal (a); (2) why this window, and were other settings tried (b); (3) is ARC at 1B a skill measure (c); (4) three seeds and one shared baseline; (5) a second judge on the sampled answers and a human read; (6) a second family and a larger size; (7) whether the result depends on adapter rank, loss weighting or dose (the q checkpoint was never evaluated; the loss plateaus by step 2-3); (8) the OLMo/Qwen contrast's cause. Gate A is an entry condition only: it says nothing about traceability, and the design (one answer style across eight units; loss-dependent unit weights; a fit that completes in the first steps) makes a diffuse Gate B reading the expected outcome (I).
