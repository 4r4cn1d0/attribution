**Assessment:** these sources further narrow the residual contribution. Generic cost/correlation allocation, shared vector observations, and adaptive proxy fallback cannot themselves distinguish the proposed deletion method.

**MultiPPI:** [Multiple-Prediction-Powered Inference](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.27414v1), identified as ICLR 2026 in its [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27414), explicitly estimates a linear functional of a vector mean from selected jointly observed coordinate subsets. Costs may be non-additive. Sections 3–4 optimize observation allocations and estimator weights using covariance; Theorem 2 gives known-covariance minimax optimality, while Theorem 3 supplies asymptotic normality for the estimated-covariance procedure. Thus, treating one retrain as a shared-cost vector of query scores, including correlated references, lies within its generic observation/cost language. This mapping is my inference, not its reported deletion application. Its target is one linear functional under fixed allocation, rather than simultaneous, anytime-valid three-way decisions over adaptively chosen deletion requests. That distinction leaves work, but “exploit shared vector observations” alone is no longer a defensible proposed novelty direction.

**R-AutoEval+:** [Adaptive Prediction-Powered AutoEval with Reliability and Efficiency Guarantees](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18659), confirmed in [NeurIPS 2025 proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/e8e81e35728f7559e42ceb265e104d36-Abstract-Conference.html), directly occupies adaptive safe proxy reliance. Equations 10–13 mix corrected observations over proxy weights including zero, maintaining valid betting evidence while shifting toward direct observations when helpful. Crucially, distinguish finite-sample testing reliability from Theorem 3's asymptotic sample-complexity comparison as the error level tends to zero; this is not a blanket finite-budget, never-worse runtime guarantee. Bounded deletion effects can motivate an adaptation, but the paper evaluates model risk, not deletion requests.

**PROBE:** [Best-Arm Identification with Generative Proxy](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.06879v1), July 2026 preprint, already learns unknown proxy correlation while certifying residual variance and allocating future samples. Its theorem obtains fixed-confidence identification with oracle-like variance/gap dependence plus calibration cost. The exact model assumes independent arms, bivariate Gaussian reward/proxy pairs, unit marginal variances, and known proxy means (§2.2); these assumptions do not automatically hold for bounded deletion effects or shared retrains. It is adjacent, not an exact solution. Nor does it establish cost-free fallback: §5.1 reports overhead when correlation is zero.

**Revision implication:** benchmark against these mechanisms where applicable and specify the remaining technical obstacle before proposing another algorithm. A plausible residual concerns joint threshold decisions with dependent, shared-cost retraining outcomes under weaker assumptions, but these sources do not establish that residual's novelty. The original verdict remains: novelty unestablished, not disproved.
