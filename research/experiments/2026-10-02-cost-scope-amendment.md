# Separate decision cost from the extra research comparator

2 October 2026, before heldout execution or any claimed economic result.
Non-author read-only review: GPT /root/minimal_date_audit on the archived first
StageB source. The lead accepts the following correction. Implementation and
verification remain pending; existing complete totals are preserved.

The first implementation adds a final reference a0 run when an edit is chosen.
Choosing a0 itself already supplies that comparator. Including this extra
research run in a decision-cost gate imposes a choice-dependent measurement
surcharge. It is real expenditure and must remain in the research bill, but is
not required to make and deploy the edit decision.

Report three scopes:

1. **Selection plus one freshly trained and audited chosen model.** Charge setup,
   features, training/validation observations, attribution and prerequisites,
   every failed attempt/retry, and one fresh chosen-model operation, including
   when the chosen model is a0. Keep the full chosen-model operation, including
   its frozen evaluation suite. Do not subtract engine stage sums from external
   walltime and pretend that gives an exact training-only deployment cost.
2. **Additional research reference and sensitivity work.** The extra final a0
   training/evaluation when an edit is chosen, and separately recorded audit or
   sensitivity work. Chosen-model evaluation stages are also displayed, without
   asserting that those internal timings partition the external timer exactly.
3. **Total research expenditure.** All actual physical work, development/tuning,
   setup, technical failures and provider expenditure, with physical shared work
   deduplicated. Counterfactual per-policy costs are reconstructions, not separate
   invoices. Each hypothetical policy still pays once for every artifact it uses.

The prospective scientific-workload budget and worst-case two-run final reserve
remain identical for both methods. Do not release the reference reserve after
seeing which method chosea0. This compares policies under equal scientific-workload
quotes and separately reports selection-plus-chosen-model costs; it is not exact
equality of realized costs or an optimized decision-only budget.

For the initial economic interpretation use scope1 and also show the unamortized
one-off total obtained by adding each method's development/tuning cost. No
unstated amortization. Display the10% cost tolerance as the linear paired margin
T_b=Cplus_b-1.10*Cminus_b, with separate raw cost differences. The corresponding
one-off margin adds Dplus-1.10*Dminus. Report uncertainty on these margins rather
than comparing an upper difference bound against an uncertain plug-in baseline
mean and calling that a joint guarantee. A practical economic claim needs both
cost scopes to be acceptable; lack of precision remains inconclusive. Walltime
intervals remain model-dependent because operation time has no known finite bound.

Setup must be charged once and conservatively: exclude reference-only setup only
when identifiable from actual receipts. Do not remove common setup merely because
its first physical load happened during a reference call. Missing or interrupted
timings retain lower bounds/unknowns; no invented zero future cost.

This amendment does not establish production savings, a stronger H2 result or
an attribution benefit. It removes a research-cost confound before evaluation.
