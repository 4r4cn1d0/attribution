## Summary

I request $3,000 for a separately recorded extension of research on safety erosion after benign fine-tuning. Does training-data attribution improve the choice of data repair beyond strong classifiers and short training runs, after charging its full cost? Both selectors receive the same permitted metadata/classifier inputs and equal compute budgets; the arm without attribution can buy extra trials. I have built the pipeline and observed a 45.1 percentage-point mean rise in an automated Unsafe measure across three OLMo-2-1B seeds. Repair pilots and a small human read exposed over-refusal and measurement limits; no attribution benefit is established. The extension would add fresh menus/seeds or a targeted scale comparison after a funded base phase and reviewed design. It aims to inform safer model adaptation, with indirect relevance to advanced-AI risk. Outputs include an empirical result, cost ledger and manuscript, including negative findings.

## Project goals

Freeze a feasible comparison with independent held-out menus, fresh seeds/prompts, strong cheap repairs and gradient/length/format controls. Charge attribution, classifiers, reference training, trials and final-model evaluation; distinguish information gains from decision gains after cost. Allocate this tranche to distinct new workloads after the base phase. Success means an interpretable, reproducible comparison with honest precision and measurement limits, not necessarily an attribution win. The practical path to impact is evidence about which diagnostics justify their cost when maintaining safety during model adaptation.
