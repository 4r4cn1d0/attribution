# Attribution research

Exploratory work on whether training-data attribution improves finite safety-data
edit decisions beyond short training runs, metadata and content classifiers,
after accounting for the full cost.

- [Current research question](research/FOCUS.md)
- [Rigor and novelty evidence ledger](research/review/EDGE_CHECKS.md)
- [Verified StageA experiment engine](experiments/safety_selection/README.md)
- [Measurement audit and limitations](research/experiments/2026-10-02-measurement-audit.md)
- [Preserved backup inventory](backups/2026-10-02/README.md)
- [Runpod incident and recovery prerequisites](research/experiments/2026-10-02-runpod-credit-incident.md)

The four-step initial study is incomplete. Earlier calibration/evaluation results
are preserved; the expanded development matrix was interrupted before its results
were retrieved. There are no heldout policy-comparison results or established
novelty, practical safety benefit or retraining-cost savings.

This is the private working repository. An anonymous review package is prepared
separately with a fresh history, restricted contents and neutral author metadata.
Historical coupling work remains preserved under `pilot/` and `research/pilot/`;
it answers a different question from the current study.
